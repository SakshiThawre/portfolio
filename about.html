About Me

A little about my background, and how I'm approaching my move into Data Science.

I'm Sakshi Thawre, a Computer Science & Engineering student at LNCT Group of Colleges (2022–2026), based in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. Over the course of my degree, I've developed a strong interest in Data Science and Machine Learning — I enjoy working with data, exploring patterns, and understanding how raw numbers can be turned into meaningful insights.

I'm currently building my Data Science foundations through structured, practical learning — studying core concepts, working through practice datasets, and applying what I learn through case studies and small projects. Alongside this, I've also gained hands-on web development experience through two internships, which taught me a lot about building real, working applications and collaborating on practical problems.

I'm a fresher, and I'm upfront about that — my focus right now is on consistent, honest learning: understanding the fundamentals properly, practicing on real datasets, and steadily building toward a career in Data Science, Machine Learning and AI.
